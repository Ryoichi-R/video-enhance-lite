# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good faith, welcome differing levels of
experience, and keep technical discussion focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed
to protect the project community. For conduct concerns, use GitHub's content-reporting and
blocking tools. Do not use Private Vulnerability Reporting for conduct disputes; that channel is
reserved for product security vulnerabilities.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used to represent
the project.
